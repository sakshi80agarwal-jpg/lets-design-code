UI/UX CASE STUDY

SavvyCart

A mobile shopping assistant designed to help users discover products, track prices, and make smarter purchasing decisions.

MY ROLE UI/UX Designer
PROJECT TYPE Mobile App Concept
TOOLS Figma
DURATION 4 Weeks
01

Project Overview

SavvyCart is a mobile shopping assistant designed to simplify online shopping by bringing product discovery, price tracking, notifications, and purchasing into one intuitive experience.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Type

Mobile App Concept

Tools

Figma

Duration

4 Weeks

02
THE GOAL

Making shopping smarter.

The goal was to help users discover products easily, track prices, stay informed about price drops, and make purchasing decisions with less effort.

PROJECT SCOPE
  • Product discovery and navigation
  • Price-tracking visibility
  • Saved and tracked products
  • Notification experience
  • Mobile usability and hierarchy
03 THE PROBLEM

Finding the right time to buy.

Shoppers often miss discounts because they repeatedly check prices or revisit shopping platforms. Manual monitoring can become time-consuming, while wishlists and carts may not clearly communicate price changes.

01

Manual Monitoring

Users repeatedly revisit products to check whether prices have changed.

02

Missed Discounts

Price drops can easily go unnoticed without timely information.

03

Scattered Tracking

Keeping track of multiple products across platforms adds unnecessary effort.

04 CRAFTED SOLUTION

From discovery to decision.

SavvyCart brings product discovery, tracking, price-change monitoring, notifications, and purchasing together in one simple flow.

01

Discover

Search and explore products easily.

02

Save

Save products you want to monitor.

03

Track

Monitor price changes in one place.

04

Decide

Receive notifications when prices drop.

05

Research & Analysis

01

Competitive Analysis

Existing e-commerce experiences were studied to understand how product discovery, wishlists, and purchasing are handled.

02

Use Cases

Key use cases included searching, viewing products, saving items, tracking prices, notifications, and purchasing.

03

Pain Points

Forgetting saved products, manually monitoring prices, missing discounts, and switching between platforms were key pain points.
