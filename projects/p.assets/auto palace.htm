ABOUT AUTO PALACE

Serving Pune Since 1972

For over four decades, Auto Palace has been helping customers buy, sell, and manage two-wheelers and four-wheelers with confidence.

Built on trust, transparency, and customer-first values, we continue to serve Pune's automotive community through three generations of the Agarwal family.

Founded by Shri Suresh Agarwal in 1972, Auto Palace began with a simple mission—to provide honest guidance and reliable solutions for vehicle buyers and sellers. Today, the legacy is proudly carried forward by Prafulla Agarwal, Pawan Agarwal, and Shivam Agarwal, continuing the same commitment to quality service and long-term customer relationships.

Beyond vehicle buying and selling, Auto Palace assists customers with RC transfers, ownership transfers, RTO-related services, and documentation support, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free experience at every step.
